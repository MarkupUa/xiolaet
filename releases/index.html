Explore the Music of Xiolaet

Welcome to the official discography of Xiolaet, an alien pop artist bringing an unfamiliar language into the world of modern pop music.

Her releases combine electropop, dance-pop and K-pop-inspired energy with expressive vocals and melodic songwriting. Each song offers a different way to experience her sound, from rhythmic movement to moments of emotional connection.

The words may be unfamiliar, but the melodies are made to stay with you.

Explore her singles, listen on your favourite streaming platform and discover the visual stories that accompany the music.

LIREVA

↗

Lireva is Xiolaet's first official release.

Its title comes from her original melodic language and evokes the idea of a response to a distant call. The song introduces her mysterious voice and the emotional world that begins to unfold through her music.

Listen to Lireva on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music and other streaming platforms, or watch the official music visualizer.
