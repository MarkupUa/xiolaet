About Lireva

Lireva is the debut single from Xiolaet, released on November 13, 2026.

It introduces a voice singing in an unfamiliar melodic language and a musical world where connection does not depend on understanding every word.

The song marks the beginning of Xiolaet's journey through alien pop, drawing on the melodic appeal of contemporary pop and the atmosphere of futuristic electronic music.

It is the first invitation to listen more closely.

What Does Lireva Mean?

The title Lireva comes from Xiolaet's original melodic language.

Its intended meaning is connected to the idea of answering or responding — a reply to something heard, felt or remembered.

That idea gives the song its central image: a signal travelling across an unknown distance, searching for someone who might recognise it.

The full meaning does not need to be explained in a single translation. The melody, the voice and the feeling are part of the message.

Perhaps Lireva is a call. Perhaps it is already the answer.

The Sound of Lireva

Lireva introduces the musical identity of Xiolaet through a meeting of melody, electronic pop textures and vocals performed in her own language.

Rather than relying on familiar words to tell its story, the song invites listeners to pay attention to the shape of each phrase, the movement of the rhythm and the emotion behind the voice.

It is music designed to connect before it is translated.
