About Xiolaet

A little unfamiliar. Impossible to mistake.

Xiolaet / pronounced Ksee-oh-LAH-et

Who Is Xiolaet?

Xiolaet (pronounced Ksee-oh-LAH-et) is an alien pop artist with a voice that crosses the distance between the unfamiliar and the deeply personal.

Her language is mysterious. Her presence raises questions. Her music offers something more immediate: the feeling that someone, somewhere, understands.

There are still many things about Xiolaet that remain unknown. She has chosen to begin with the music rather than explanations.

And perhaps that is the best way to discover her.

The Music — Futuristic Pop With Emotion

Xiolaet's musical world is rooted in modern electropop, dance-pop and K-pop-inspired idol-pop.

Her sound combines electronic production with melody-driven songwriting. Rhythmic energy gives the songs movement, while expressive vocal lines carry the emotions at their centre.

Some moments are bright and energetic. Others are intimate, reflective or filled with the feeling of distance.

What connects them is a focus on memorable melodies, emotional contrast and songs that invite another listen.

Xiolaet's music is not about being different for the sake of being different. It is about finding a familiar feeling in an unexpected sound.

An Original Language Beyond Translation

One of Xiolaet's most distinctive features is the language she sings in.

It is an original melodic language with its own vocabulary, sounds and patterns. Its flowing syllables are shaped to work naturally with rhythm and melody, allowing her voice to communicate in a way that goes beyond conventional lyrics.

The language is not simply a collection of random sounds. Words have meanings, and phrases are created to express ideas and emotions within the songs.

For listeners, understanding every word is not a requirement.

A repeated phrase can become familiar. A melody can make a meaning feel clear before it is explained. And sometimes, the most powerful part of a song is the emotion that needs no translation.

The name of Xiolaet's language has not yet been revealed.

A Voice Worth Following

Every new song is an opportunity to hear another side of Xiolaet.

Her music explores connection, distance, belonging and the search for understanding. These ideas appear through melody, rhythm and the language that gives her songs their distinctive character.

Her debut single, Lireva, is the first place to begin.

Where the next song leads is still unknown.
