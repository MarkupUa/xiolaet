Meet Xiolaet

A voice with
its own gravity.

Some voices are familiar from the very first note. Xiolaet’s is not.

Her language is unknown to most of those who hear it. Her appearance raises questions she rarely answers. Yet the emotions in her music need no explanation.

For now, she has chosen to let the songs speak first.

Listen a little closer.

Her music brings together electropop, dance-pop and the expressive energy of idol-pop. Her voice stays at the centre: a held note, a sudden change in tone, a phrase you want to hear again.

The words belong to her language. What you hear in them can be your own. You don’t need a translation to recognise longing, resolve, or the wish to be understood.
